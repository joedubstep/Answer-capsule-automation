# Inbox Desk

## What it does

It reads your new email twice a day so you don't have to keep checking. It sorts it and writes the replies for you.
Every reply waits in Gmail as a draft. Nothing is ever sent to anyone but you.

- **Cold email replies.** Many owners read your first email as a family asking for a room ("we have rooms, are
  you looking for memory care?"). The desk spots that and drafts a short reply that clears it up and offers the
  one-page plan, with your booking link. Other replies get their own pattern: "who is this?", "call me", "we're
  full", "talk to our director". Auto-replies and opt-outs get no draft.
- **Plan requests.** When a home says yes to the plan, the draft already has the one-page plan attached as a PDF.
  It covers their home, the 3 things you would set up in the first 90 days, one thing they can try this week, and
  your booking link. Beds and care type are on it only when a state license record or the home's own website
  shows them, with the link. There is no price on it, no stats and no promises.
- **Google Voice texts.** A text from someone new gets a short draft reply. Sending it texts them back. Texts from
  your own business lines (your own tests and agent replies) are left alone.
- **Client mail** (AGC, Splendor, your legacy client). It drafts the answer. AGC partner mail comes through
  GoHighLevel, so that draft has no To line: copy it into the AGC conversation.
- **Coach emails** (Senior Living Mastery, Acquisition.com, Lead Suite and the coaches you list). The lesson goes
  into your Learnings table in Notion: a short summary, the key lines word for word, and where to use it
  (JP delivery, Offer, Sales calls, Campaigns, Ads, Ops). You tick Applied when you use it.
- **Data-sale and vendor mail.** It drafts the answer from your Answer Bank page. Anything marked "verify" there
  comes to you instead.
- **Opt-outs.** If someone opted out but still has an E code or an open deal in GoHighLevel, it tells you to close it.

Every draft goes through the Copy Checker first, so no dashes, no price and no stats ever reach a draft.

## When it runs

Weekdays at 8am and 4pm Eastern. You can also say "inbox desk now". Say "inbox desk backlog" once to answer every
cold reply since September 23 that nobody answered yet.

## Where the output lands

- **Gmail drafts**, in each email's own thread.
- **One short email to you**, only when something is new: "Inbox Desk: 3 drafts ready (E24, E21, Maple House
  plan), 1 needs you". No email means nothing needs you.
- **Notion Learnings** for coach lessons. **Google Drive** keeps a copy of every plan PDF.
- A **Desk/Done** label on every email it finished, so it never does the same one twice.

## What you do with it

1. Open the digest email.
2. Open each draft in Gmail. Read it, change anything you want, and press Send. Check the From line: send it from
   the address they wrote to. The digest names it for each draft.
3. Do the "needs you" lines (for example, mark an opted-out deal lost in GoHighLevel).
4. Now and then, open Learnings in Notion and tick Applied on what you used.

## Minutes saved

About 2 hours a week. That is the inbox checks several times a day, writing each cold reply by hand, building and
sending the plans homes asked for, and copying coach lessons into notes. It also stops replies from sitting
unanswered: 22 owners replied well and only 1 meeting was booked.

## Good to know

- It never sends a draft and never emails anyone but you.
- It only reads GoHighLevel. It never changes a record.
- People who write from a personal address (a coach on Gmail, a client's own email) go on the private "Inbox Desk
  settings" page in Notion, never in this code.
- If the booking link is missing from the settings, the drafts that need it are held and the digest says so.

## How to pause it

In claude.ai, open Routines, find "Inbox Desk" and switch it off. Or ask Claude: "pause the Inbox Desk routine".
